Meet the Founder

Meet the Founder

Akpomovine Jerrison

Founder, Blaze Studio

Blaze Studio was founded by Akpomovine Jerrison with a simple idea: businesses should be able to access good digital products without unnecessary complexity.

Jerrison works across web development, product design, automation, and digital technology, working directly with clients to understand their needs and turn them into practical solutions.

As a founder-led studio, Blaze Studio keeps the process direct. Clients communicate with the person responsible for understanding the project, shaping the solution, and making sure the final product delivers what was intended.
