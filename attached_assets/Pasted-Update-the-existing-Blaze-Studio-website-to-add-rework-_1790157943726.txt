## PAGE GOAL

The About page should establish three things quickly:

1. What Blaze Studio is.
2. Who founded it.
3. Why a potential international client can trust the studio enough to start a conversation.

The page should feel confident and professional without exaggerating the size, experience, client count, or capabilities of the company.

Do NOT invent:

* Clients
* Testimonials
* Awards
* Years of experience
* Team members
* Offices
* Revenue
* Partnerships
* Certifications
* Statistics
* Case studies
